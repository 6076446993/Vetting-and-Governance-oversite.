# Oversight Contract Requirements

## Purpose

Independent Oversight governs custody eligibility and organism governance.

Oversight is not scientific verification.

Oversight is not learning.

Oversight is not runtime execution.

---

## Required Contract Objects

RegisteredComponent

OversightDecision

CustodyEligibility

StopOrder

ClearOrder

OversightSignature

---

## Core Principles

Oversight approval is not scientific proof.

Custody eligibility is not verification.

Registration is not trust.

Oversight remains independent from Crucible execution.
