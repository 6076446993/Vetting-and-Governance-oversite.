# Trustworthy AI Oversight

This repository implements the Nexus Trustworthy AI Governance Contract within its existing governance and independent-vetting authority.

## Required controls
- Preserve independent review and the exact version reviewed.
- Record explicit decision, reason, evidence references, lineage, and applicable signature.
- Preserve material dissent, unresolved uncertainty/risk, failed checks, and conflicts/disclosures where applicable.
- Maintain STOP/CLEAR authority and ancestry without silently bypassing or erasing prior STOP state.
- Keep governance approval distinct from scientific or technical verification.
- Make decisions challengeable and auditable through retained evidence and decision lineage.
- Fail closed when required authority, evidence, lineage, identity, or signature is missing.
- Support safe rejection, revocation, supersession, recovery, and decommissioning within existing governance authority.
- Review trustworthy-AI controls when governed lifecycle, AI-provider, or repository-participation boundaries materially change.

This document does not transfer Crucible verification authority or custody authority to oversight.
